# Screening Rules

## Middle-Term Trend Screening

Screening should not only find strong stories. It should identify candidates with observable trend conditions.

For each candidate, include:

- Industry or theme fit.
- Company quality filter.
- Current trend state.
- Entry observation condition.
- Risk or invalidation condition.
- Whether it deserves watchlist conversion.

## Candidate Language

Use:

- "适合观察"
- "等待回踩确认"
- "等待放量突破确认"
- "暂不提高优先级"
- "风险偏高，先不纳入"

Avoid:

- "可以买"
- "确定会涨"
- "必须加仓"
