# Monthly Portfolio Review

## Purpose

Close the loop across weekly reviews, monthly outcomes, method evolution, and forward trend assessment. It runs automatically on the first day of each month at the configured default time, currently `09:00`, to review the previous month, unless the same monthly report was already generated by a manual user trigger.

The monthly report should identify what worked, what failed, why it failed, what should be improved after user confirmation, and how the next 1-3 month trend scenarios may affect the user's portfolio.

## Inputs

- `AGENTS.md`
- `config/portfolio.yaml`
- `config/strategy.yaml`
- `config/paths.yaml`
- `config/data_contracts.yaml`
- `config/evidence_policy.yaml`
- `config/product_metrics.yaml`
- `knowledge/methods/*.md`
- `knowledge/product_metrics_protocol.md`
- Weekly reports in `reports/weekly/`
- Daily reports in `reports/daily/` when weekly reports are missing
- Decision and method-change logs in `memory/`

## Workflow

1. Identify the month and collect weekly reports.
2. If running automatically on day 1, review the previous calendar month.
3. Check whether a same-month report already exists. If it was generated by a manual trigger, do not duplicate it unless the user confirms refresh.
4. Fetch or verify actual monthly market and portfolio data.
5. Audit weekly views, strategy execution quality, data quality, and whether user effort/noise was reduced.
6. Identify recurring mistakes and method gaps; separate method errors, data errors, behavior errors, and normal market noise.
7. Build next 1-3 month trend scenarios with triggers, portfolio impact, and invalidation signals; avoid deterministic single-path prediction.
8. Propose method, source, schedule, or watch-rule improvements.
9. Save the report to `reports/monthly/YYYY-MM.md`.
10. Append proposed changes to `memory/method_changes.jsonl` with enough context for later confirmation.

## Report Structure

```markdown
# YYYY-MM 月复盘

## 一、本月核心结论
## 二、本月实际市场表现
## 三、持仓月度表现
## 四、周复盘观点回测
## 五、策略执行质量评估
## 六、明显错误与系统改进建议
## 七、未来 1-3 个月走势判断

| 走势情景 | 倾向 | 核心理由 | 触发条件 | 组合影响 | 应对策略 | 验证/失效信号 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 八、后续持仓关注点
## 九、下月检查清单
## 十、数据来源与备注
```

## Style Rules

- Write in Chinese.
- Be retrospective before predictive.
- Propose improvements, but require user confirmation before editing strategy or methods.
- Explain validation and invalidation signals for all forward-looking views.
- Trend judgment is required, but must be scenario-based rather than a deterministic prediction.
- Include a product-effectiveness check when data exists: alerts, duplicate suppression, repeated refreshes, rule-based action ratio, and evidence coverage.
